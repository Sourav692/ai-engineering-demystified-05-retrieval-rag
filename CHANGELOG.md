## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-29

Extracted from the `AI-ENGINEERING-DEMYSTIFIED` monorepo at tag `pre-multirepo-split-2026-09` as a
fresh snapshot (no history). The foundational RAG phase (`02_Core/04_Retrieval_and_RAG/`, including its
`RAG_Curriculum/` and `RAG_Production_Course/` tracks) and the foundational notebooks of the
`Comprehensive_RAG_Techniques` anthology were merged into one ordered sequence of 13 modules and
102 notebooks, with shared data under `data/sample/`, the `helpers` LLM factory under `src/helpers`,
and every moved data, image and link path repaired. 81 source files were deferred and stay in the
monorepo for later repositories — 73 to repository 09 (agentic, corrective, adaptive, self, graph and
other advanced RAG, plus the rest of the anthology) and 8 to repository 13 / the separate
`Agent_Evaluation_Demystified` repository (evaluation, serving). The full mapping is in
`docs/content-inventory.csv`.
